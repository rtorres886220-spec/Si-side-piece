Product overview · Sidekick for Chrome

A browser agent that proves what it does.

01The problem

Most browser agents are a chat window with unchecked hands.

They answer fluently, act confidently, and fail quietly, with no guarantee a quote is real, a number wasn't invented, or a sensitive field stayed untouched.

Typical agent

Trusts itself.

The model picks the target, writes the answer, and grades its own work. Mistakes pass straight through.

Sidekick

Checks itself.

The model proposes. Independent checks decide what is allowed to happen and what counts as an answer.

02How it works

Understand. Act. Verify.

Every request follows the same simple arc, so you always know what Sidekick did and why.

Understand

Sidekick reads the page you are on and works out what you are asking for.

Act

It answers, or takes the steps on the page, only within the tabs you have given it.

Verify

Before anything counts, it is checked against the page itself. What can't be backed up doesn't get through.

03Capabilities

Built for the real web, and for your judgment.

Citations

Click to see the source.

Answers carry quotes from the page. Click a citation and the page scrolls to and highlights the exact text.

Forms

Knows when to stop and ask.

Sidekick can work through multi-step forms, and it pauses for you on personal or ambiguous questions rather than guessing.

Scope

Works where you point it.

Group the tabs you want Sidekick to use. Everything outside that group stays untouched.

Control

You stay in charge.

Stop a task at any moment with a single key, and approve anything consequential before it happens.

05Self-diagnosis

When something is off, it tells you why.

A built-in health check looks at your setup from inside the extension and offers one-click fixes, so you are never left guessing.

  • Connection

    CHECKED

    Confirms your key is set up and working.

  • Current page

    CHECKED

    Tells you when a page can't be used, and why.

  • Readiness

    CHECKED

    Spots and repairs tabs that were open before install.

06Trust & safety

Limits that don't depend on anyone behaving.

Scope

Only where you allow

Sidekick acts only in the tabs you have given it.

Sensitive inputs

Off-limits by design

Payment and credential fields are not something Sidekick will fill in for you.

Evidence

Grounded in the page

Quotes and figures in an answer are checked against the page they came from.

Ownership

Your key, your browser

You bring your own API key. It is stored locally in Chrome.
